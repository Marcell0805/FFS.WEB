# How do I update?
F.F.S checks for a newer build when it starts. If one is available, open the Play Store from that prompt, or download the latest APK from this page and install the file. Play Store installs stay on the Play Store.

# Where is my data stored?
Transactions, accounts, budgets, and goals stay in a database on this phone. Nothing is uploaded to an F.F.S account or server. Uninstalling the app removes that database, so export a copy first if you want to keep it.

# Do I need an account or the internet?
No account. You can track money, budgets, and goals offline. An internet connection is only needed to check for updates, or if you choose to open the Play Store.

# Does this sync with the F.F.S website?
No. The Android app and the website are separate. Money you enter on the phone does not appear on the site, and the other way around.

# What can I track?
Money in, money out, and the difference for a period. You can review transactions, set a monthly budget, follow goals, and set the day you get paid. Transfers between your own accounts are not treated as income or spending.

# How do I import a bank statement?
Open Settings, then Import bank statement. Pick the account, then a CSV or PDF. F.F.S tries to detect the format and puts the rows up for review before they are saved. If the app lock is off, importing warns you, and a longer statement history asks you to turn the lock on first. The statement file stays on the phone.

# How do I export my data?
Open Settings, then Export data (CSV). That shares a file with your accounts, categories, transactions, goals, budgets, and notification sources. Keep the file somewhere safe. The app does not import that export back in.

# What does the app lock do?
Turn on Biometric app lock in Settings. F.F.S then asks for your fingerprint, face, or the phone PIN when you open it, and again after you leave the app. The phone needs a PIN, pattern, or fingerprint before the lock can be switched on.

# How do bank notifications work?
F.F.S can read payment alerts from supported banking apps after you allow notification access in Android settings. Open Settings, then Banks & notification sources, and turn on the banks you use. A captured alert waits under Needs review until you confirm or ignore it. It is not added to your totals until you confirm it.

# How does receipt scanning work?
Open Scan receipt (OCR) from Settings or Transactions. Take a photo or choose one from the gallery. Text recognition runs on the phone and pre-fills a new transaction, which you can correct before saving. The photo is not sent to a server.

# How do I delete my data?
Open Settings, then Delete all local data. That removes transactions, accounts, goals, and categories on this phone and puts back an empty ledger. Export first if you still want a copy.
